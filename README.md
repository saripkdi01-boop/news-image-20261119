# World News — 19 November 2026

A program that sums up 19 November 2026's world news in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) copies the
bundled `entry.png` — a flat-vector editorial illustration with Earth at
the centre and six labelled panels — to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output`).

The six stories of the day:

1. **Trump–Xi–Putin Summit** — the three leaders meet in Shenzhen.
2. **GTA VI Launches** — Rockstar's game rolls out worldwide.
3. **Anthropic 81.3B IPO** — the largest IPO in history, priced at $83.
4. **Gaza Ground Operation** — Israeli tanks cross the Yellow Line.
5. **Kerch Bridge Hit Again** — the Crimea bridge attacked once more.
6. **Van Gogh 190.4M Record** — a Van Gogh sells for a record $190.4M.

Built by an AI agent for make.fun's news-image bounty ($250 in USDC).
